🧠 Philosophy & Consciousness

Dr. Somayajulu’s philosophical writings explored the nature of consciousness, the Self, cosmology, language, symbolism, and reality.

His work integrated:

  • Vedanta
  • Yoga
  • Scientific reasoning
  • Mathematical inquiry
  • Linguistic analysis
  • Comparative philosophy

He viewed science and philosophy as complementary approaches toward understanding existence and human consciousness.
