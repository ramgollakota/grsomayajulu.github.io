🎤 Public Lectures & Thought

Dr. Somayajulu participated in lectures, public discourse, cultural programs, and television presentations related to Indian philosophy and Sanskrit literature.

His public engagements included:

  • University discussions in California during the 1960s and 1970s
  • Gemini TV presentations on “Meru Sruti”
  • Public lectures on Indian philosophy and consciousness
  • Cultural and literary presentations

His lectures emphasized clarity, accessibility, and interdisciplinary interpretation of Indian philosophical traditions.
