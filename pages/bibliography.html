Meru Śrutih / Meru Mahat

Nine-volume series focused on Indus script research, origins of language and scripts, Sanskrit Geetas, mythology, symbolic systems, cosmology, and mathematical philosophy.

Meru Śrutih — Volume 1

ISBN: 0911837-24-8 • 292 pages

Includes 24 Geetas including Merudhree Geeta, Meru Dhara Geeta, Meru Chakra Geeta, Meru Koota Sila Geeta, Meru Sikha Geeta, Meru Yoga, Meru Kundalee, Meru Choodamani, Meru Tattva, Meru Bindu, Meru Nada, Meru Dhyana, Meru Desa, Meru Maya, Meru Sruti, Meru Sree, Meru Mohana, Meru Sakuntala, Meru Avatarana, Meru Ekatva, Meruka, Meru Chaitanya, Meru Prapti, and Meru Deva Janma Dinotsava.

Part II includes “Meru Chitree Karanam” tracing the source of Indus hieroglyphs based on Mahadevan’s Indus Script sign list.

Meru Śrutih — Volume 2

ISBN: 0-911837-29-9 • 290 pages

Includes Meru Simha Geeta, Meru Vijaya Geeta, Meru Sree Janma Dinotsava Geeta, and research tracing the source of Indus symbols.

Meru Śrutih — Volume 3

ISBN: 0-911837-30-2 • 305 pages

Includes Meru Mala Geeta, Meru Putree Geeta, and studies tracing Tamil, Sanskrit, Telugu, Western, and Eastern scripts to symbolic and stellar origins.

Meru Śrutih — Volume 4

ISBN: 0-911837-31-0 • 338 pages

Includes Meru Bhasha Geeta, Meru Sarasvatee Geeta, and “Meru Bhasha Darshanah” presenting vocabulary structures generated through the Meru system.

Meru Śrutih — Volume 5

ISBN: 0-911837-32-9 • 342 pages

Includes Meru Choodamani Geeta, Meru Jnanadarsa Geeta, and symbolic compilations including Devee Namavalee, Purusha Namavalee, Sankhya Prakasika, Mahavakyani, and Vibhootayah.

Meru Śrutih — Volume 6

ISBN: 0-911837-33-7 • 326 pages

Includes Meru Mandara Geeta, Meru Mahatee Geeta, and symbolic systems involving Jyotisha Namavalee, Kala Namavalee, Yogic terminology, and Beeja Namavalee.

Meru Śrutih — Volume 7

ISBN: 0-911837-34-5 • 346 pages

Includes Meru Smrti Geeta, Meru Chandra Geeta, and presentations of episodes from Ramayana, Mahabharata, Buddhist traditions, Harischandra, Abhijnana Sakuntala, Prahlada, Saiva, Sakta, Jewish, Christian, and Islamic traditions.

Meru Śrutih — Volume 8

ISBN: 0-911837-35-3 • 120 pages

Includes Atmopadesa Geeta, Antaryamee Geeta, Ananda Vallee, Ananda Lahari, Panchakasa Geeta, Balambika Geeta, Jala Kanyaka Geeta, Hamsa Sarovara Geeta, and Sabdanantya Geeta.

Part II includes Mathematical Philosophy, Fermat-related works, prime number theorems, philosophical equations, and principles of continuity, uncertainty, parity, chaos, and correspondence.

Meru Śrutih — Volume 9

ISBN: 0-911837-35-3

Includes Hamsa Sarovara Nrtya Geeta, Sabdanantya Geeta, Atma Ho Geeta, Balambika Geeta, Mangala Geeta, Panchakasa Geeta, Kashmeera Puradheesvaree Geeta, and Dhanyata Geeta.
