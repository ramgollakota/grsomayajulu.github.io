Dr. G. R. Somayajulu ("Aniruddha") made interdisciplinary contributions spanning Sanskrit literature, Indian philosophy, scientific research, comparative religion, linguistic inquiry, music, and cultural preservation.

Philosophical and Sanskrit Scholarship

  • 27-volume Upanishads and philosophical literature project
  • Rigorous Sanskrit transliteration framework
  • Over 200 Sanskrit Geetas and compositions

Science and Interdisciplinary Research

  • Scientific research in thermodynamics and chemistry
  • Research into the Indus script and language origins
  • Philosophical writings on consciousness and the Self
