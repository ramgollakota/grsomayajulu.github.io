𓂀 Indus Script Research

Dr. Somayajulu conducted interdisciplinary research into the origins of the Indus script, language formation, symbolism, and ancient civilization.

His investigations explored relationships between:

  • Indus symbols
  • Astronomical patterns
  • Constellations and mythology
  • Sanskrit and world languages
  • Ancient symbolic systems

His work combined linguistic analysis, scientific reasoning, and comparative symbolic interpretation.
