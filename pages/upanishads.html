SANSKRIT & PHILOSOPHICAL WORKS

🕉 108 Upanishads Project

Rigorous interpretation, transliteration, classification, and philosophical synthesis of the Upanishadic tradition.

The 108 Upanishads Project represents one of the central intellectual and literary contributions of Dr. G. R. Somayajulu (“Aniruddha”).

The work was not merely a translation effort, but a rigorous analytical and interpretative treatment of Indian philosophical literature combining:

  • Original Sanskrit source material
  • Precise transliteration systems
  • English translations and renderings
  • Cross-tradition philosophical synthesis
  • Scientific and analytical interpretation

The project spans 27 volumes covering Vedanta, Yoga, Samkhya, Nyaya, Mimamsa, Bhakti, Sannyasa, and related philosophical traditions.

Foundations of the Upanishadic Tradition

In the introductory writings to Sampoorna Mahā Prasthānam, Dr. G. R. Somayajulu presented a systematic study of the origins, classifications, cosmology, and philosophical foundations of the Upanishads.

His treatment combined traditional Sanskrit scholarship with analytical interpretation, comparative philosophy, cosmology, consciousness studies, and scientific reasoning.

The Sources of the Upanishats

From Sampoorna Mahā Prasthānam, Volume I (1999)

Discusses the origins of the Upanishads, Vedic classifications, cosmological structure, lunar mansions, and the development of the traditional enumeration of 108 Upanishads.

Classification of the Upanishats

Presents systematic grouping of the Upanishads into Mahā Vedānta, Sāmānya Vedānta, Sannyāsa, Yoga, Vaishnava, Shaiva, and Shakta traditions.

Essentialism & Unqualified Nondualism

Introduces Aniruddha’s interpretation of Vedanta emphasizing Essentialism, Oneness, consciousness, and integration of science and philosophy.

The Great Statements of Vedanta

Analytical discussion of Mahā Vākyas including “Tat Tvam Asi,” “Aham Brahmāsmi,” “Sarvam Khalvidam Brahma,” and related philosophical foundations.

Science, Cosmology & Consciousness

Explores relationships between cosmology, consciousness, space, cyclic existence, Vedantic inquiry, and scientific approaches to grand unification and reality.

The Great Seers & Philosophical Traditions

The work also examines the contributions of major philosophical seers including Yājñavalkya, Uddālaka Aruni, Kapila, Narada, Dattatreya, Maitreya, Ribhu, Paingala, and Mandookyāyana, while discussing the foundations of Vedanta, Sankhya, Yoga, Nyaya, Vaisheshika, and related systems of Indian thought.

Dr. Somayajulu’s interpretative framework emphasizes the essential unity underlying diverse philosophical traditions while preserving textual authenticity and conceptual precision.
